Selected projects

01 In development — Personal Project

Vibrocompliant Detangler

A handheld concept device for heatless 4C hair detangling using low-frequency mechanical vibration (10–40 Hz ERM motor) and a passive adaptive flex bristle array — designed to redistribute force away from high-resistance knot zones rather than pulling through them. The core principle: never fight the curl pattern. Developed from personal experience with 4C hair and independent research into tensile properties, scalp sensitivity constraints, and compliant mechanism design. Currently at Phase 1 CAD in SolidWorks, focusing on bristle geometry, TPU compliance base, and vibration isolation from the outer shell.

Compliant mechanism design Vibration engineering SolidWorks CAD Hair tribology TPU/silicone materials Force distribution Prototype sequencing

03 Academic project — EN2602 Design

Electric Motorsport Reduction Gearbox

Two-stage spur gear reduction gearbox for a prototype electric motorsport vehicle (6000 to 1500 rpm, 400 Nm output torque). Full hand calculations for gear stress, shaft static and fatigue analysis, and SKF bearing selection for 9,000-hour life. Output shaft assembly mass-optimised through iterative ANSYS FEA. Final design SLS-printed in PA2200 polyamide and physically tested on a servo-hydraulic load machine to validate FEA predictions. Manufacturing drawings produced to final production standard.

ANSYS FEA SolidWorks Gear design Fatigue analysis Bearing selection SLS prototyping Physical testing Manufacturing drawings
04 Academic project — IMechE Design Challenge

Autonomous EV Charging Robot

Led mechanical design for an autonomous robotic device simulating EV charging under tight dimensional and performance constraints. Modelled and stress-tested the chassis in SolidWorks to verify structural integrity under dynamic loads of internal components. Designed and simulated the full mechatronic system in CircuitLab. Ran iterative EVT cycles to validate the design before competition, formally reported findings, and presented the finished system to the IMechE judging panel.

Chassis design FEA / stress analysis Mechatronics CircuitLab EVT cycles Technical presentation
